## Bottom line

The **9-Oct rotation picture is stronger but more concentrated** than the previous reading.

The market is shifting toward:

> **Energy → commodities → gold/miners → agriculture**, while **healthcare remains the most reliable persistent leadership group.**

The most important *new-entry* signal is **XBI**: momentum has crossed 100, velocity is strongly accelerating, and it has 11/12 weeks of leadership persistence, but RS is still just below 100. 

The biggest **risk signal** is financials: XLF/IYF retain excellent historical persistence but have now slipped below RS 100.  

And the most interesting **early reversal** is semiconductors: SMH remains weak, but its momentum is starting to accelerate. That is **watchlist territory, not yet a buy signal**. 

**Overall: Risk-on 8/10, but increasingly commodity/energy-led rather than broad equity-growth-led.**
